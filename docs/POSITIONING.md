# The Audacity: Positioning

Single source of truth for how The Audacity describes itself. Every surface (site copy, meta descriptions, JSON-LD, `llms.txt`, `agent-manifest.json`, emails, decks, social) derives from this file. Change it here first, then propagate.

## Category

**Agentic Engineering Studio**

Use the category name verbatim, title case. Do not paraphrase it.

## One-liner

An Agentic Engineering Studio: a software house where AI agents do the engineering and senior judgment owns architecture, security and quality.

## Elevator

An Agentic Engineering Studio. AI agents build, senior engineering judgment owns architecture, security and launch readiness. We take an idea to a deployed product in weeks, not a slide deck.

## Tagline

AI agents build. One human ensures quality.

## Mission

Ship production software at studio quality and agent speed, so founders and teams get real, running products instead of prototypes, decks or hours billed.

## Definitions

- **Agentic engineering:** software designed, written, tested and shipped by orchestrated AI agents, inside human-owned architecture, code review and security gates.
- **Studio:** we sell delivered, running software (product builds, agent systems built into a business, build-and-run retainers), not hours, staff augmentation or advice.
- **Senior judgment:** the human part of the studio decides what to build, how it is architected and secured, and what is ready to ship.

## What we are not

- Not a fractional CTO practice.
- Not a staff-augmentation or body-shop agency.
- Not a no-code shop.
- Not a strategy consultancy.

## Phrasings

Approved:

- "Agentic Engineering Studio" (the category)
- "the studio" (short form in running copy)
- "software house" as a plain-English helper, at most once, near the category, for searchability
- "AI agents build. One human ensures quality."
- "agentic engineering" (the practice)

Banned (retired self-descriptions):

- "fractional CTO" / "fractional CTO practice"
- "AI-first software studio"
- "autonomous software agency"
- "AI software house" / "AI-first software house"
- "agency" as a self-description (it reads as staff augmentation)
- "Senior judgment, AI acceleration" / AI as 'acceleration' (agents do the engineering, they do not merely accelerate it)

## Writing rules

- No em-dashes or `--` as phrase separators. Use a comma, colon, parentheses or a new sentence.
- Never use the word "land" or any of its conjugations.
- No overclaiming a team: The Audacity is run by two founders working with AI agents. Never invent headcount, clients or numbers.
- The firm speaks as "we" or "the studio" in running copy. Founder names may appear on team and about surfaces (team cards, the About page, the FAQ answer to "Who does the work?", structured data) only once real inputs exist: real names, roles, photos and links supplied by the founders. Never invent a name, role, bio or photo.

## Scope

This file governs The Audacity surfaces only. The founder's personal surfaces (miketineo.com, CV, LinkedIn) are out of scope and keep their own positioning.
